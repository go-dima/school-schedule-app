This file describes the staff role in the school scheduling system, including what can be done

#### Staff Role abilities
- A staff can view schedules of all children - the dropdown in the schedule will show list of children with who has a selected schedule
- An admin will be able to view schedules of all children - the dropdown in the schedule will show list of children with who has a selected schedule (same as staff)
- A staff will be able to edit the schedule for a child
- A staff can not manage users (no user management page or approvals)
- A staff can edit lessons (same as admin)

#### UI changes for roles
- The sidebar for a staff will have the following links:
    - Schedule
    - Class management
    - Profile settings
    - The user management section will be removed from the sidebar
- currentRoleAlert should be removed from the top of the schedule page
- The profile-dropdown-user element will have another line under name width the role of the user (Staff, Admin, Parent, Child)
- The profile dropdown now shows the user's role through the border color:
  - 🔴 Red border for admin
  - 🔵 Blue border for staff
  - 🟢 Green border for parent
  - 🟡 Yellow border for child
